### D1: originality

The paper must make an identifiable contribution rather than merely repackage established findings: it should specify the gap it addresses, distinguish its framework, evidence, or perspective from prior work, and engage that prior work substantively when explaining the contribution.

### D2: methodological_rigor

The research design must answer the stated question with an appropriate and justified method, suitable data or samples, reproducible analysis detail, and an explicit account of limitations; because this dimension is mandatory, defects that undermine design fit or replication cannot be treated as optional polish.

### D3: evidence_sufficiency

The paper's factual and substantive claims must be supported by adequate, field-appropriate citations or data, and its interpretations must remain within the evidence rather than relying on unsupported assertions, selective evidence, or conclusions stronger than the results permit.

### D4: argument_coherence

The paper must sustain a consistent thesis from its opening through its conclusion, connect sections with intelligible logical transitions, answer the research question it poses, and avoid gaps or contradictions among its principal claims.

### D5: writing_quality

The prose must be clear, precise, readable, and appropriate for an academic venue, with controlled grammar, consistent formatting and citation style, and figures or tables integrated into the written discussion where applicable.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: originality

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: I will look for a specific contribution and gap statement, substantive differentiation from cited prior work, and discussion that explains what new framework, evidence, or perspective the paper adds.
what_triggers_block: I will assign block if the contribution is vague or absent, prior work is merely listed rather than engaged, or known conclusions are restated in a new setting without added evidence.
what_triggers_warn: I will assign warn if a contribution is present but only weakly distinguished from prior work or if engagement with relevant sources is materially uneven.

### D2: methodological_rigor

dimension_id: D2
what_to_look_for: I will look for a named and justified method that fits the research question, appropriate data or samples, reproducible analysis steps, and explicit limitations.
what_triggers_block: I will assign block if the method does not fit the research question, the design has significant flaws, limitations are absent, or the analysis description is not reproducible.
what_triggers_warn: I will assign warn if the method is basically sound but important details are missing, choices are insufficiently justified, or limitations are named without meaningful engagement.

### D3: evidence_sufficiency

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: I will look for support for every factual or substantive claim, field-appropriate source quality, and discussion inferences that do not exceed the reported evidence.
what_triggers_block: I will assign block if multiple core claims lack evidence, the argument depends excessively on one source, inferences exceed the data, or favorable evidence is cherry-picked.
what_triggers_warn: I will assign warn if core claims are supported but some secondary claims are not or if citation support is materially uneven across sections.

### D4: argument_coherence

dimension_id: D4
what_to_look_for: I will look for a conclusion that answers the introductory research question, clear section-to-section transitions, and a thesis that remains consistent across the abstract, introduction, and conclusion.
what_triggers_block: I will assign block if there are multiple logical gaps, the conclusion is disconnected from the research question, or passages contradict one another on central points.
what_triggers_warn: I will assign warn if the overall reasoning holds but some transitions are forced or minor inconsistencies appear among section-level claims.

### D5: writing_quality

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: I will look for a consistently academic register, rare grammatical errors, internally consistent citation formatting, and in-text discussion of every figure and table used.
what_triggers_block: I will assign block if the paper is difficult to understand, contains numerous grammatical errors, uses a colloquial register, or applies citation formatting inconsistently across the manuscript.
what_triggers_warn: I will assign warn if the paper remains readable but has several grammar or word-choice problems, overly long paragraphs, or minor citation-format inconsistencies.
