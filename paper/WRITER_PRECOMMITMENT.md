# Phase 4a Writer Pre-commitment

Paper metadata: *Authenticated Relationship Routing Mitigates External-Identity Attacks in LLM-Agent Collectives*; LLM-agent security, multi-agent systems, and trustworthy AI; target 8,000 words.

Contract: `academic_paper/writer_full/v1`.

## Acceptance Criteria Paraphrase

### D1: section_completeness

Every section approved in the outline will be present in the draft, including the declarations and limitations required for submission. No placeholder will stand in for missing substantive text.

### D2: citation_density

Claims about prior research, standards, or external facts will carry a verified IEEE citation. Study-specific statements will point to the frozen protocol, accepted data, or analysis artifacts, and unsupported generalizations will be removed or qualified.

### D3: argument_blueprint_fidelity

Each section will follow the approved claim-evidence-reasoning structure. Evidence will support the stated claim, and interpretation will return to the research question without exceeding the locked evidentiary boundaries.

### D4: total_word_count

The body will remain within ten percent of the 8,000-word target, excluding abstracts, references, tables, captions, and appendices.

### D5: per_section_word_count

Each principal section will remain within approximately fifteen percent of its approved allocation unless a small redistribution is needed to preserve clarity while keeping the total compliant.

### D6: paragraph_structure

Most body paragraphs will open with a clear claim, present evidence, explain its relevance, and connect to the surrounding argument. Methods and results paragraphs may use procedural or report-first structures where those better fit their function.

### D7: register_consistency

The paper will maintain precise computer-science terminology, a formal academic register, and consistent names for deployments, policies, outcomes, and statistical quantities.
