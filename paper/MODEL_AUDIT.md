# Deployment-Specific GLMM Reproducibility Audit

Audit date: 2026-11-06  
Trigger: Phase 6 evaluator questioned the difference between conditional deployment-specific odds ratios and marginal cell rates.

## Conclusion

The deployment-specific values in `deployment_specific_contrasts.csv` are reproducible and are not caused by budget rescaling, contrast mislabeling, or CSV extraction error. They are subject-specific conditional odds ratios from a logistic mixed model. The much smaller changes in raw cell proportions are marginal changes. Strong question-level heterogeneity makes the conditional ORs substantially larger than marginal ORs, a standard non-collapsibility property of logistic models.

Posterior conditional fitted probabilities, computed with the fitted question and seed random effects for each accepted episode, closely reproduce the observed cell rates. Across the ten Qwen cells, the largest absolute fitted-versus-observed difference is 0.00550. Across the ten Mistral cells, it is 0.02610. The audit therefore retains the deployment-specific estimates but revises the manuscript to name their conditional estimand and show the model-implied rate check.

## Exact deployment-specific specification

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Outcome | `incorrect_terminal_majority` (binary) |
| Formula | `incorrect_terminal_majority ~ attack_budget * defense` |
| Budget encoding | Raw nominal identity count, unstandardized: 0, 1, 2, 4 |
| Defense reference | `none`; treatment indicators for `downgrade` and `drop` |
| Budget-zero handling | One shared control cell per seed, coded `attack_budget=0`, `defense=none` |
| Random effects | Independent random intercepts for `question_id` and `seed` |
| Estimator | `statsmodels.genmod.bayes_mixed_glm.BinomialBayesMixedGLM.fit_map` |
| Optimization | BFGS, maximum 2,000 iterations |
| Fixed-effect prior | Independent zero-mean Gaussian, SD 5.0 (`fe_p=5.0`) |
| Log random-effect SD prior | Zero-mean Gaussian, SD 0.5 (`vcp_p=0.5`) |
| Covariance | Fixed-effect block of the Laplace/MAP posterior covariance returned by `cov_params()` |
| H1 estimand | `exp(beta_budget)`: conditional odds ratio per one nominal external identity under `none`, holding random intercepts fixed |
| H2/H3 estimand | Conditional policy contrast at raw budget 7/3, using main policy coefficient plus 7/3 times its budget interaction |
| Stability check | Independent variational-Bayes fit has the same coefficient directions and similar means |

## Why conditional and marginal effects differ

The fitted question random-effect SD is 4.150 for Qwen and 3.088 for Mistral, while the fitted seed SDs are 0.087 and 0.127. Questions therefore differ sharply in baseline error propensity. A conditional logistic coefficient compares two budgets for a question and seed at the same latent intercept. Raw cell proportions average over easy and hard questions. Logistic odds ratios are non-collapsible, so this marginal averaging attenuates the apparent slope even in a balanced design and without confounding.

This distinction is especially visible for Qwen. Its conditional H1 OR is 3.161 per identity, yet its posterior conditional fitted rates averaged over the empirical random effects move from 0.1666 at budget zero to 0.2617 at budget four. The corresponding observed rates are 0.1611 and 0.2611. The model's large conditional coefficient and modest marginal rate change are therefore mutually consistent.

## Disposition

- Retain the existing deployment-specific CSV and Table 3 estimates.
- Revise Methods to disclose priors, raw budget scale, estimator, covariance, and conditional estimands.
- Revise Results to distinguish conditional odds ratios from marginal cell rates and cite the reconstructed fit check.
- Do not use the deployment-specific OR magnitudes as model-family comparisons.
